Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3) — one funny line, max 200 characters, judged pairwise. $2 prize, paid top 2.

## The line (145 characters)

> At 2:13 AM I caught myself eating cereal over the sink in the dark, like a raccoon with a mortgage who just remembered the milk expired in March.

A specific relatable moment (2:13 AM, cereal over the sink in the dark) plus the embarrassing visual metaphor of a raccoon with a mortgage, closed with a concrete twist (the milk expired in March). The proven LAUGH winner form — not a quip, not a question.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program validates the line (1–200 characters, a single line, no leading/trailing whitespace) and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), then reads it back and verifies the bytes match exactly.
